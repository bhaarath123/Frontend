Salient Features of the Indian Constitution

What makes India's constitution unique

Introduction | History | Features | Parts of Constitution | Amendment Process

Main Features

  • Longest written constitution in the world
  • Drawn from British, American, Irish, and Canadian constitutions
  • Directive Principles of State Policy
  • Universal Adult Franchise — every citizen above 18 can vote
  • Independent Judiciary with Supreme Court at the apex
  • Quasi-federal structure with strong centre
  • Single citizenship for all Indians
  • Secular state — equal respect to all religions

The Preamble

The Preamble declares India to be a sovereign, socialist, secular, and democratic republic. It secures justice, liberty, and equality for all citizens.
