Meaning of Fundamental Duties

Fundamental Duties are the responsibilities that citizens owe to the nation. They help strengthen democracy and social harmony.

Important Duties

  • Respect the Constitution
  • Respect the National Flag and Anthem
  • Protect the unity and integrity of India
  • Promote harmony and brotherhood
