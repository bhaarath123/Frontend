Background

The Constituent Assembly of India was formed in 1946. After nearly three years of debate and drafting, the Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949 and came into force on 26 January 1950.

Key Events

  1. 1946 — Constituent Assembly formed
  2. 1947 — India gained independence on 15 August
  3. 1949 — Constitution adopted on 26 November
  4. 1950 — Constitution came into effect on 26 January

Important Personalities

  • Dr. B. R. Ambedkar — Chairman of Drafting Committee
  • Dr. Rajendra Prasad — President of Constituent Assembly
  • Pt. Jawaharlal Nehru — First Prime Minister of India
