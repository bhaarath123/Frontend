Important Parts

The Constitution is divided into multiple parts. Some important parts are listed below.

  1. Part I — The Union and its Territory
  2. Part II — Citizenship
  3. Part III — Equality and Liberty
  4. Part IV — Directive Principles of State Policy
  5. Part V — The Union (Executive, Parliament, Judiciary)
  6. Part VI — The States
  7. Part VIII — The Union Territories
  8. Part IX — The Panchayats
  9. Part XI — Relations between the Union and the States
  10. Part XII — Finance, Property, Contracts and Suits
  11. Part XX — Amendment of the Constitution

Schedules of the Constitution

  • 1st Schedule — States and Union Territories
  • 7th Schedule — Union, State, and Concurrent Lists
  • 9th Schedule — Validation of certain acts and regulations
  • 10th Schedule — Anti-defection provisions
