The Constitution of India

The Constitution of India is the supreme law of the country. It gives the people a framework for government, rights, duties, and justice.

Date of Adoption: 26 November 1949
Date of Enforcement: 26 January 1950

What is the Constitution?

The Constitution is a written document that defines the powers of the government, protects the rights of citizens, and explains the structure of the nation. It is the foundation of democracy in India.

It was drafted by the Drafting Committee and is admired for its balance of justice, liberty, equality, and fraternity.

Important Facts

  • Father of the Indian Constitution: Dr. B. R. Ambedkar
  • Number of Articles: 448
  • Number of Parts: 25
  • Number of Schedules: 12
  • Importance: The Constitution protects citizens and guides the government.

Quick Facts

It is a living document that grows with the nation.

India is a republic with democratic values.
